Architecture and System Solution

A Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) system for complex Hebrew insurance policies. The system features structure-aware chunking (section_aware) and E5-based vector retrieval against ChromaDB.

Business Value and Enterprise Problem Solving

Deterministic accuracy on legal contracts and exception-heavy policies

🎯

Evidence-Based Retrieval

Dealing with complex answers: retrieving exclusion clauses, deductibles, and sub-limits from the original document, with a transparent methodology for measuring performance and documenting limitations.

🚀

Integration into FinTech & Wealth Management

The engine is designed as an independent Microservice that connects directly to financial systems (such as ai-wealth-monitor), allowing policyholders and agents to get a reliable, real-time overview of all insurance coverages.

The Problem Solved: Large Language Models (LLMs) hallucinate facts when asked about specific policy details — coverage limits, deductibles, exceptions — because they don't know them. RAG solves this by dynamically retrieving the right section before generating the answer.

Two Chunking Strategies

Hypothesis: Chunking by natural sections beats mechanical chunking

fixed_size

944 chunks • 500 chars

Cuts at arbitrary boundaries — causes sections to split in the middle

...additional conditions for the policy. ## Replacement vehi ────── chunk N ────── cle service The insurer is not...

section_aware

447 chunks • ≤2,800 chars

Cuts at header boundaries — each chunk = a complete semantic unit

## Replacement vehicle service The insurer is not obligated Replacement vehicle garage arrangement Will be provided for partial damage... ────── complete chunk ──────
💡 Each chunk carries an anchor = first 80 characters — a stable citation key between both strategies

Insights, Conclusions & FAQ

Everything we learned — from the experiment to design questions

🏆

section_aware wins

On documents with clear header structure — Chunking by natural sections yields ~2.7x higher MRR than any fixed_size variant

🔑

Embedding asymmetry is critical

Both in e5 (prefixes "passage:"/"query:") and gemini (task_type=DOCUMENT/QUERY) — without distinguishing between document and query, retrieval plummets. A silent failure.

⚓

anchor > chunk_id

Using the first 80 characters as a citation key survives between both strategies — allowing a fair comparison with the same Gold Set

Embedding Model Substitution (Gemini vs e5)

We substituted only the embedding model for gemini-embedding-001 (API, 768d) — the rest of the pipeline is identical. Two independent metrics: Retrieval (Hit@5, MRR below) and Answers (10 manual samples, see below).

Embedding ModelCorrectIncorrectΔ

✨ Improvement: Q5 and Q6 ("table row" premiums) moved from Incorrect to Correct with gemini. ⬇️ Regression: Q2 (liability limit) — gemini retrieved a semantically close but incorrect chunk. ↔️ Shared failures (Q1, Q4): Numerical values inside tables weren't retrieved accurately in section_aware — a chunking limitation, not embedding.

🎯 Where gemini improved the answer

Q5 — What is the annual premium for side mirrors and headlights coverage?

🔴 e5: "The model did not find the premium"

🟢 gemini: "The annual premium for headlights and side mirrors coverage is 58 NIS" ✓

Q6 — What is the premium for towing services included in the 3rd party insurance?

🔴 e5: "The model stated there is no separate premium"

🟢 gemini: "The premium for towing services included in 3rd party insurance is 274 NIS" ✓

⬇️ Where gemini failed

Q2 — What is the maximum liability limit for third-party property damage insurance?

🟢 e5: "2,833,953 NIS" ✓ (Retrieved the main table)

🔴 gemini: "600,000 NIS per event; 1,200,000 NIS for all events" — retrieved the compensation limits table, a semantically close but incorrect field.

Takeaway: Even strong semantic search can fail when multiple semantically close tables exist in the same document. Both numbers are factually correct on their own — but not relevant to the question.

💪

Strengths

Both models perform well on complex factual and contractual questions (5/5 in Q3, Q7-Q10)

⚠️

Remaining Weaknesses

Even with gemini, Q1+Q4 (numerical values from tables) still fail — section_aware chunks are cut at the header level and don't create independent retrieval units for table rows

🔮

Next Steps

gemini already added +10%. Hybrid retrieval (BM25 + dense) or table-aware chunking can capture the remaining table rows

Which embedding model is used in production?

The academic baseline is multilingual-e5-large (local, open-source, allows reproducibility). In the Substitution Experiment, it was proven that gemini-embedding-001 (API, 768d) improves retrieval by +11% Hit@5 and +10% Correctness in answers — two independent metrics. In practice: gemini for production, e5 as a secure and private baseline.

python -m venv .venv && .venv\Scripts\activate
